What lives under a single oak leaf

12 min read
[Photo]

Turn one over in late spring and you find a small city: aphids farming sap, the ants that tend them, and the spotted beetles that come for both. Most of it happens within a few centimetres.

The beetles arrive first. A single one eats dozens of aphids in a day, and the larvae eat more.
