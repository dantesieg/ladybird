Ladybird

An independent browser, with your tabs always in sight.

Two rows at the top hold everything: your open tabs, side by side like on a desktop, and under them back, the address, reload and the menu. The rest of the screen is the page.

Principles
Tabs in sight
A tab strip above the address. The current tab is outlined and widest. Every target 40 px or more.
Page over chrome
The bar shrinks to the site name while you scroll, and comes back when you scroll up.
Violet means action
The accent is used only for the thing to do next, never for decoration.
